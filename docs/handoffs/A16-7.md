### 2. Balance is by decision opportunities, not lap count

2026 registry numbers, `decision_interval_m = 70.0`:

| Circuit | laps | length_m | decisions/race |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| monza | 53 | 5793 | 4386.1 |
| spa | 44 | 7004 | 4402.5 |
| monaco | 78 | 3337 | 3718.4 |
| mexico-city | 71 | 4304 | 4365.5 |

monza/spa weight ratio **0.996278** against a lap-count ratio of **1.204545**:
the 53-lap circuit does not outweigh the 44-lap one. Because every F1 race is
about the same distance, real-circuit weights are nearly uniform, which is the
intended effect and a weak discriminator on its own. On stand-in circuits with
declared numbers (long: 8000 m x 40 laps; short: 3000 m x 90 laps) the
separation is visible:

expected 0.4991 / 0.5009.

### 5. `PlanningWorld(...)` == `PlanningWorld.from_scenario(...)`

`from_scenario` is exactly `load_bundle(scenario_id)` plus the same four field
assignments — it does nothing the direct construction skips. **No restoration
or classmethod extension was needed**, and `planning/rollout.py` was not
touched. Verified for all four synthetic scenarios at `seed=777`: the frozen
dataclasses compare equal, including `bundle_hash`, `ego_car_id`,
`rival_car_id` and `checkpoint_ids`.

The direct construction is strictly better for a real-circuit scenario: it
hands the planner the **already-resolved** bundle, so re-simulation runs
against the same conditions tape the episode runs on. `from_scenario` would
re-read the scenario from disk and get an unbound (static-reference)
environment. If a future caller wants the classmethod on a real-circuit
scenario, `PlanningWorld.from_scenario` needs a resolved-bundle parameter;
`planning/planner.py:174` still uses the classmethod and would silently plan
against static conditions for a scenario with a `conditions_id`. Flagged below.

hash on the bundle; asserting the full hash inside `describes` will fail.

Tapes are always loaded with `allow_network=False` on this path.

The same attachment path on a synthetic sketch, isolating the weather binding
from geometry readiness:

```

synthetic-wet-gusty  tape sha256:13733f9ffb35…  bundle.environment_hash equal=True
```
