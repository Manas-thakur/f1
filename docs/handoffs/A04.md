### Deviations from the specification's literal signatures

1. `check_plan(plan, state, context)` gained a keyword-only `manifest=`. The
   spec's three-argument form still works but runs degraded: without the pack
   the checker can only use the single resolved scalar the context carries, so
   the speed curve, zone geometry and per-lap allowance cannot be re-derived
   independently. `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` requires the checker to load the same
   immutable pack, so passing the manifest is the intended call.
2. `resolve_context` gained keyword-only `session_id`, `thermal_derate` and
   `unknown_conditions`. `RuleContext.session_id` is a required contract field
   with no source in the spec's argument list, and the other two carry pack
   information that has no home on the frozen `RuleManifest`.

No contract change was needed, so no `A04-contract-proposal.md` was written.

## The checker's reintegration model (declared, auditable, arguable)

`ProfileSegment` gives an energy budget over a stretch of track, not a power
trace. To reintegrate it the checker had to choose a distribution, and it states
it rather than hiding it:

* deployment power follows the shape of the applicable ceiling,
  `P_dep(s) = k_d · C_reg(v(s))`, with `k_d` fixed per segment so that
  `∫P_dep dt = requested_budget_j` at the deployment bus;
* battery-gain harvest follows speed, `P_har(s) = k_h · v(s)`, with `k_h` fixed
  per segment so that `∫P_har dt = harvest_target_j`;
* charge-bus ledger = battery gain ÷ `CheckerState.charge_bus_efficiency`;
* battery drain = `P_dep` ÷ `CheckerState.discharge_efficiency`.

Subdivision happens at segment boundaries, speed-profile breakpoints,
power-curve breakpoint crossings, lap rollovers and every detection/activation/
checkpoint line, then `CheckerConfig.substeps_per_interval` (default 8) equal
slices inside each. Integration is trapezoidal in time and **exact** for a
piecewise-constant speed profile; the trapezoid restarts at a step
discontinuity so no energy is smeared across an instantaneous speed change.
Every check reads the resulting trace, so interior extrema are first-class.

The checker never reads `plan.constraint_result` or `plan.solver_status`.
`test_checker.py::test_solver_declared_pass_is_still_rejected` proves it.

## Worked expected values (a sample, so the numbers can be re-derived)

* **Interior energy minimum.** 1000→3000 m, 50 m/s then 100 m/s at the 2000 m
  break: 20.0 s + 10.0 s. Ceiling-time integral 350 kW·20 s + 100 kW·10 s =
  8.0 MJ, so an 800 kJ budget gives `k_d = 0.1` → 35 kW then 10 kW.
  Speed-time integral 2000 m, so a 600 kJ harvest gives `k_h = 300` → 15 kW then
  30 kW. Net −20 kW for 20 s then +20 kW for 10 s. From 380 kJ: **−20 kJ at
  2000 m**, recovering to 180 kJ. Both endpoints legal, the interior is not.
  Margin `−20 000 J`.
* **Charge-bus recharge.** 600 kJ of battery gain at 0.8 charge-bus efficiency
  costs 750 kJ on the bus. With 7.9 MJ already used, the battery-gain reading
  would be exactly 8.5 MJ (margin 0, a pass); the CU-K DC reading is 8.65 MJ →
  margin **`−150 000 J`**. The test asserts both numbers so the bus distinction
  is what fails the plan.
* **Impossible ramp.** 20 kW → 350 kW over a 0.3 s window = 1 100 000 W/s
  against 700 000 W/s → margin **`−400 000 W/s`**, while the ceiling check
  passes at exactly 0 W margin.
* **Thermal derate.** 363.15 K is half way between 353.15 K and 373.15 K, so the
  factor is `1.0 + 0.5·(0.6 − 1.0) = 0.8` and 350 kW becomes 280 kW. A 300 kW
  plan passes `power_ceiling` (+50 kW) and fails `thermal_derate` (−20 kW).
* **Crossing interpolation.** 1500 → 2000 m over 10.0 → 12.0 s puts the 1600 m
  detection line at 10.4 s and the 1900 m activation line at 11.6 s.

## Assumptions

1. **Unknown-condition applicability.** A pack's unresolved conditions each
   carry `critical` and `scope` (`session`, `advisory`, `overtake_permission`,
   `sector:<id>`). Only applicable ones reach `RuleContext.unknown_conditions`
   and empty the admissible set; **all** of them reach
   `RuleManifest.unknown_conditions` and `ConstraintResult.unresolved_conditions`,
   so a pack cannot hide one by calling it advisory. An unrecognised scope is
   treated as applicable. Marking a condition advisory is itself an unreviewed
   assertion, stated in the pack note.
2. **Ramp scope.** The power-change limit is applied to driver-commanded profile
   transitions (entry into the plan, and each segment boundary), not to the
   power unit tracking the speed curve within a segment. A different reading of
   C5.12 would change this.
3. **Overtake margin unit.** `overtake_eligibility` reports metres: headroom of
   permitted activation zone left after the request, or minus the whole
   requested length when no permission exists. The zone is
   `[activation line, next checkpoint)`.
4. **Execution lead time** is a product constraint, not a regulation. Required
   lead is `CheckerState.driver_reaction_time_s`; both the run-up time and each
   segment's `execution_window_s` must clear it.
5. **Efficiencies live on `CheckerState`**, not on the rule pack: charge-bus and
   discharge efficiency are car properties owned by A03/A05. They default to 1.0,
   which means "not modelled here", not "lossless in reality".
6. **Missing temperature is unknown, not 1.0.** If a pack configures a derate
   model and no temperature is available, `thermal_derate_factor` and
   `deployment_ceiling_w` are `None`, the context becomes unknown and
   `admissible_profiles` is empty. Integrators must supply `temperature_k` or
   accept suppressed advice.
7. **`harvest_target_j` is battery energy gain**, converted to the charge bus by
   the checker. If the planner means bus energy, that is a contract ambiguity to
   settle (see below).
8. **Race control does not move the ruleset hash.** `ruleset_hash` covers
   levels 1–2 only; flags are an event stream.

## Known limitations / defects

1. **Torque limiting is not implemented.** `RULE_MATRIX.md` groups it with
   temperature limits; only temperature derating exists. Coverage row is
   `review_required`.
2. **The reintegration distribution is a modelling choice**, not an observation.
   A driver who deploys differently within a segment can produce a trajectory
   the checker did not predict. The choice is documented in `checker.py` and
   should be reconciled with A03's simulator and A06's planner.
3. **Trapezoid error on a non-constant speed profile.** Exact for piecewise
   constant speed; second-order otherwise. Raise
   `CheckerConfig.substeps_per_interval` if a caller needs tighter interior
   extremum resolution on a strongly varying profile. No adaptive refinement.
4. **Interior extrema are located by subdivision**, not by root-finding on the
   net-power sign change. With the default grid an extremum strictly inside a
   sub-slice is approximated rather than solved for.
5. **The overtake zone model is one activation line followed by one checkpoint.**
   Multiple overlapping zones per lap are not modelled.
6. **`RuleContext.event_pack_hash` currently repeats the manifest hash** when an
   event level exists. There is no separate frozen sub-object to hash.
7. **`recovery_ceiling_w` is set to the absolute DC ceiling.** The packs declare
   no separate recovery ceiling; no check consumes this field yet.
8. **Rival/sporting judgement is out of scope.** The checker reports feasibility
   limits; it asserts nothing about steward judgement.

## Required integration actions (coordinator)

1. **Pre-existing, not caused by this module — conftest name collision.**
   `./.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pytest tests` fails with
   `tests/data/test_capabilities.py::test_ingesting_a_drs_field_archives_it_without_producing_a_channel`
   → `ImportError: cannot import name 'public_config' from 'conftest'
   (…/tests/numerics/conftest.py)`. Two module directories without `__init__.py`
   both define `conftest.py`, so `sys.modules["conftest"]` is whichever loaded
   last, and a module-level `from conftest import …` picks up the wrong one.
   `tests/rules/` ships an `__init__.py` and uses `from .conftest import …`, so
   it does not participate; A02 (`tests/data`) and A03 (`tests/numerics`) need
   the same fix or a shared `tests/` conftest.
2. **Confirm `ProfileSegment.harvest_target_j` semantics** with A06. This module
   reads it as battery energy gain and converts to the charge bus. If the
   planner writes bus energy, the checker's conversion double-counts and one
   side must change.
3. **Wire `CheckerState.charge_bus_efficiency` / `discharge_efficiency`** from
   the A03 car configuration instead of the 1.0 defaults, otherwise the
   recharge-allowance ledger under-reports bus energy.
4. **A06 must call `check_plan(..., manifest=pack.manifest)`**, not the
   three-argument form, or the speed-curve, zone and allowance checks degrade.
5. **A08 must store `ConstraintResult.ruleset_hash` on the selection** and
   compare it at execution; `test_pack_change.py::test_checker_reports_the_new_hash_after_a_pack_update`
   demonstrates the rejection criterion.
6. **A08/A09 must surface `unknown` distinctly from `fail`.** An unknown check
   has `margin=None` and the aggregate is `unknown`; rendering that as a failed
   check or as zero would be wrong.
7. **A03/A05 own the eligibility inputs.** `EligibilityMachine.advance` needs
   `gap_condition_met`; passing `None` (unresolved) is correct and must not be
   replaced with `False` for convenience.
8. **Pack promotion is the coordinator's.** `load_rule_pack` refuses a
   non-synthetic pack that is not `reviewed`, and refuses an id starting with
   `synthetic` that does not declare `synthetic: true`. A worker cannot activate
   an unreviewed non-synthetic pack.
